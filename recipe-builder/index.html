Recipe Builder features

Find and manage recipes

Use the search box to find a recipe by name or ingredient. Choose Edit to update a recipe, then choose Ok and Save. Choose Delete and confirm to remove one. Changes to a shared album affect everyone who uses it.

Pick One

Choose Pick One for a random suggestion from the selected album. Select the suggested recipe’s name to show it in the recipe list.

Download a backup

Choose Download Recipes to download a JSON backup of your current collection. Downloading is not the same as saving; save changes in the editor too.

Share an album

Open an album you own and choose Share Number. Give the number only to people you trust. They must sign in, choose Join, and enter the number. Anyone who joins can change recipes in that shared album. You cannot share albums you joined.

AI recipe tools

Recipe Builder uses AI to read recipe cards and suggest recipes from food photos or dish names. Review and correct AI results before saving. AI features have a daily usage limit per person.

Your recipe collection and the editor require sign-in.

Add your first recipe: step by step

  1. Open your collection and set up a category

    Select Open Recipe Builder and sign in if prompted. In the editor, open your album panel. Under Categories, choose Add Category and enter any label that helps you organize recipes. It could be an author, such as “Grandma,” or a holiday, meal, or recipe type. Categories are yours to customize. You cannot delete a category while recipes are assigned to it; edit those recipes and move them to another category first.

  2. Choose how to add the recipe

    Use Scan a written recipe and choose a photo of a handwritten or printed recipe card to have AI read it. Use Create a recipe from a food photo and choose a picture of a prepared dish to get an AI-generated suggestion. Or enter a dish name under Create a recipe from a name and choose Generate Recipe. To enter everything yourself, choose Add Recipe.

  3. Review and organize

    Check the recipe name and choose a category from the Category menu. Review the ingredients, add or remove rows as needed, and set each row’s position. Cost is optional. Entering a recipe by hand? Fill in these same details.

  4. Finish the recipe, then save the collection

    Choose Ok to close the recipe form and add the recipe to the page. This does not save the collection yet. Choose Save on the page and wait for the save confirmation. Your recipe is saved to the selected album.

Review AI suggestions

AI tries to put ingredients under Top and instructions under Bottom, but it can make mistakes. A food photo cannot show the exact ingredients. Check the recipe against your source, correct the placement, and add or remove details before saving.
