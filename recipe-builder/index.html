How Recipe Builder works

  1. Choose how to start

    Use Scan a written recipe for a handwritten or printed recipe, or Create a recipe from a food photo for a picture of a dish. You can also type a dish name to get a suggested recipe, or add one manually. Restaurant-inspired recipes may not match the original.

  2. Review the details

    Check the suggested recipe name and ingredients, make any corrections, and confirm the recipe.

  3. Organize and save

    Arrange your recipes into categories and use the Save button to save your changes. Search your collection when you need a favorite again.

More ways to use your collection

Download your recipes or use Pick One when you need an idea.

To share your own album, choose Share Number in the album panel and give the number to someone you trust. The button is not shown for albums you join. They can sign in, choose Join, and enter it. People who join can update the shared recipes.

Recipe Builder uses AI to help interpret images and suggest recipes. Review the results before saving. AI features have a daily usage limit per person.
